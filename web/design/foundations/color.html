Four states, no more. There is no pending: a run is running from the moment it is recorded.
Three rules this palette exists to enforce.

1. unreachable is not failed. Magenta, not red — the machine was never reached, which is a network or inventory problem, not a playbook one. Collapsing them sends the reader to the wrong file.

2. changed is loud. Amber, at full weight — it means something on a real machine was modified. It is the answer to the only question that matters after a run, and GET /runs?changed_min= exists precisely to filter on it. Muting it to an "info" grey would bury the signal.

3. interrupted is slate, not red. ADR-0031: a dying runner does not pronounce a verdict. Colouring it as a failure would say your playbook failed about a playbook that said nothing.
